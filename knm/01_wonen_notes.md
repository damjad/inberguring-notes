## 1. Een Huis Regelen (Finding a Home)

### Social vs Private Rental
| Sector | Dutch | For whom | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social rental | **Sociale huur** | Low income | Low (regulated) |
| Private rental | **Vrije sector** | Higher income | Market rate |
| Owner-occupied | **Koopwoning** | Anyone with mortgage | Purchase price |

### Sociale Huurwoning — How It Works
- Register with a **woningbouwvereniging** (= **woningcorporatie**) — they own and rent the social housing
- Eligibility depends on: **inkomen** (not too high), **aantal personen**, sometimes **leeftijd** (some housing for 70+ or 80+)
- Register via websites: **Woningnet**, **Wonizi**, **WoonLinburg** — registration can cost €10–30/year and must be renewed annually
- **Wachtlijst** rule: longest registered person gets the next available home — register as early as possible
- Long waits in big cities: **Amsterdam, Utrecht, Rotterdam, Den Haag**; shorter waits in smaller towns
- When a home becomes available: you receive a **letter** from the woningcorporatie → respond immediately
- Newly arrived in NL: gemeente may assign you a home, unless **gezinshereniging** applies

### Urgentieverklaring (Priority Status)
- Apply at the **gemeente**
- Grants temporary priority on the waiting list
- Valid reasons: **ziekte**, **scheiden met kinderen**, **dakloos zijn** (homeless), **huis gesloopt** (home demolished)
- Rules and costs differ per gemeente

### Vrije Sector Huren
- Rent from a woningcorporatie or **huisbaas** (private landlord)
- Find via: advertenties (krant / website) or **makelaar**

### Huurcontract — What's In It
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Huurprijs** | Monthly rent amount |
| **Servicekosten** | Extra charges (e.g. building cleaning, maintenance) |
| **Opzegtermijn** | Usually **1 month**, sometimes 2 |
| **Onderhoud** | See table below |

### Repair Responsibilities
| Type | Who pays |
|---|---|
| Groot onderhoud (lekkage, kapotte verwarming) | **Verhuurder** (landlord) |
| Klein onderhoud (kapotte lamp, wasmachine) | **Huurder** (tenant) |

### Huurder Rights (Huurwet)
- Right to **veilig wonen** — no mold or leaks
- Huur may **not increase excessively**
- Verhuurder must do all **major repairs**
- Right to **rust en privacy**

### Scenario Rules
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Looking for social housing | Register at **woningcorporatie** (Woningnet etc.) as early as possible |
| Need housing urgently (illness/homeless/divorce) | Apply for **urgentieverklaring** at gemeente |
| Received a letter about available woning | **Respond immediately** |
| Rent too high for your income | Apply for **huurtoeslag** at www.toeslagen.nl |
| Problems with landlord (bad maintenance/rent dispute) | → **Huurcommissie** or **Juridisch Loket** (gratis advies) |
| Neighbour conflict | Talk first → **buurtbemiddeling / wijkteam** → politie (0900-8844) |

## 2. Een Huis Kopen (Buying a Home)

### Key Facts
- Most buyers need a **hypotheek** (mortgage loan from a bank)
- Higher income → higher hypotheek possible; some incomes are too low for any hypotheek
- Bank usually requires a **vast inkomen** (permanent job contract) before granting a hypotheek
- Rente (interest) differs per bank — compare before choosing
- Pay monthly installments + rente to the bank

### Hypotheekrenteaftrek
- You can **deduct** the mortgage interest (rente) from your income in your **belastingaangifte**
- This means you pay less belasting — the belastingdienst pays back a portion

### Notaris
- At the end of the purchase process, buyer and seller go to the **notaris**
- The notaris is where you sign the **contract** for the house and the hypotheek

### Verzekeringen bij Kopen of Huren
| Insurance | Dutch | Covers | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liability | **Aansprakelijkheidsverzekering** | Damage you or your child causes to others | All residents |
| Home contents | **Inboedelverzekering** | Damage or theft inside your home | All residents |
| Building | **Woonhuisverzekering** | Damage to the building (fire, storm, water) | **Koopwoning only** |

## 3. Energie en Communicatie

### Key Facts
- **You** arrange your own energy (gas, electricity) — not the woningcorporatie or huisbaas
- You choose your own **energiebedrijf** (e.g. Nuon, Eneco)
- Compare prices at **www.energieprijzen.nl**
- When you move: take **meterstand** (gas, electricity, water) — take a photo and report to your new leverancier
- **No registration with a leverancier = no energy delivery**

### Energy Contract Types
| Type | Price | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| **Vast energiecontract** | Fixed for 1 or 3 years | More certainty |
| **Variabel energiecontract** | Price changes (can go up or down) | Sometimes cheaper |

- You may usually switch providers (**overstappen**) once per year
- Watch **opzegtermijnen** and **boetes** when switching early

### Energierekening
| Bill | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Termijnbedrag** | Monthly estimated payment |
| **Jaarrekening** | Annual settlement — extra to pay or money back |

### If You Don't Pay Your Energy Bill
1. First: **waarschuwing** (warning) from energiebedrijf
2. If still unpaid: **energy is cut off (afgesloten)**

### Energy Problems
| Problem | Action |
|---|---|
| Can find/fix yourself (e.g. close hoofdkraan for gas) | Fix it yourself |
| Can't fix, unsure if isolated | Check if **buren** have same problem |
| Can't fix, affects you only | Contact **energiebedrijf** |
| **Dangerous** (gas leak, electrical fault) | Contact energiebedrijf **immediately** |
| No gas at all | Call **Nationaal Storingsnummer** |

### Saving Energy (Zuinig met Energie)
Benefits: better for **milieu** and **cheaper** for you
- Turn off **verwarming** and **lampen** when not home
- Buy **A-label** apparaten
- Don't overuse high-energy appliances (wasmachine, droger, vaatwasser)
- Use **duurzame energie** where possible: windenergie, zonneenergie

### TV en Telefoon
| Service | Details |
|---|---|
| **NPO 1, 2, 3** | Free — overheid pays; no subscription needed |
| **Commercial TV** (RTL, Veronica, SBS) | Via kabel or internet; **abonnement** needed |
| **Internet + TV + radio** | Can be combined in one abonnement |
| **Mobiele telefoon** | Via provider (Ziggo, Tele2, KPN); choose **beltegoed** or **abonnement** |

## 4. Onderhoud en Woonomgeving (Maintenance & Living Environment)

### Afvalscheiding (Waste Separation)
| Waste type | Container |
|---|---|
| Glass | **Glasbak** |
| Paper | **Papierbak** |
| Vegetables / fruit / garden | **GFT-container** (Groente, Fruit, Tuin) |
| Plastic | **Plasticbak** |
| Chemical waste (paint, batteries) | **Klein chemisch afval** — bring to designated drop-off point |

### Afval Rules
- Gemeente collects waste on **fixed days** (differs per buurt)
- Putting waste out on the **wrong day** = **boete**
- Everyone pays **milieubelasting + reinigingsheffing** (waste/cleaning tax)
- Low income → can apply for **ontheffing** from this tax
- Rules differ per gemeente — check **gemeente website**, **afvalkalender**, or **Afvalwijzer app**

### Why Afvalscheiding Matters
- Recycling = materials reused → less CO₂ uitstoot, less new grondstoffen needed, cleaner air/water/soil

### Living Environment Expectations
- Dutch neighbours expect a **netjes en verzorgd** (tidy) house and garden
- No afval in the garden; grass mowed

### Neighbour Disputes
| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1. Talk | Speak directly with neighbour |
| 2. Mediation | → **Buurtbemiddeling / wijkteam** |
| 3. Non-urgent police | Call **0900-8844** |
| 4. Serious threats (**bedreiging**) | Call **politie** directly |

## Key Organisations Summary

| Organisation | Role |
|---|---|
| **Woningbouwvereniging / Woningcorporatie** | Own and rent social housing |
| **Gemeente** | Urgentieverklaring, assign housing to new arrivals, afval rules |
| **Huurcommissie** | Disputes about rent level or poor maintenance |
| **Juridisch Loket** | Free legal advice for tenant disputes |
| **Buurtbemiddeling / Wijkteam** | Mediation for neighbour conflicts |
| **Makelaar** | Help buying or finding private rental homes |
| **Notaris** | Handles house purchase contract and hypotheek signing |
| **Belastingdienst** | Huurtoeslag, hypotheekrenteaftrek |
