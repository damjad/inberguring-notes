## 1. De Rechtsstaat en de Grondwet

### Key Facts
- Nederland is a **rechtsstaat** — everyone (individuals and government) must follow the law
- The most important law is the **grondwet** (constitution)
- The grondwet contains **fundamental rights** that apply to all people in the Netherlands

### Fundamental Rights in the Grondwet
| Right | Dutch | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Freedom of expression | **Vrijheid van meningsuiting** | You may always give your opinion, also in media (TV, radio) |
| Freedom of religion | **Vrijheid van godsdienst** | Everyone may have their own religion and gather with people of the same religion |
| Freedom of education | **Vrijheid van onderwijs** | Parents may choose a school for their child; anyone may start their own school |
| Non-discrimination | **Discriminatie is verboden** | All people have equal rights: men/women, homo/hetero, religious/non-religious |

### Conflicting Rights
- When two rights conflict in a specific situation, a **rechter** (judge) decides which right takes precedence

### Women's Rights (Zelfbeschikkingsrecht)
- Women in the Netherlands have the right to:
  - Choose whether and whom they marry
  - Decide whether to have children
  - Use contraception or abortion
  - Choose what to study and whether/what to work
- This flows from the broader principle: **zelfbeschikkingsrecht** — everyone may decide about their own body and life

## 2. Scheiding tussen de Wet en Religie

### Key Facts
- Everyone in the Netherlands is free in their belief, but **Dutch law always prevails**
- In case of conflict between law and religion → **wet gaat voor** (law wins)
- Nederland is a **neutrale rechtsstaat** — not bound to any religion; no religion determines the laws
- **Kerken en moskeeën** cannot decide what happens in Dutch society

### What Is Forbidden Even for Religious Reasons
| Forbidden act | Dutch |
|---|---|
| Intimidation | Intimidatie |
| Denying girls access to education | Meisjes geen onderwijs geven |
| Discrimination based on religion or sexual orientation | Discriminatie op geloof of seksuele voorkeur |

### Practical Principles
- Religious clothing is **allowed**, as long as it does not impair safety
- School lessons must be **neutral and inclusive**
- Laws on marriage, rights, and care apply to **everyone**

### Scenario Rules
| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Religious school refuses to hire homosexual teacher | Rechter rules this is **discriminatie** — law wins |
| Religion forbids girls from attending school | **Forbidden** under Dutch law |
| Conflict between religious rule and Dutch law | **Dutch law takes precedence** in all cases |

## 3. Verboden in Nederland (What Is Prohibited)

### Key Facts
- **All forms of violence** are forbidden and **strafbaar** (punishable):
  - **Huiselijk geweld** (domestic violence)
  - **Eerwraak** (honour killing)
  - **Verkrachting** (rape)
- Punishment: **boete** (fine) and/or **gevangenis** (prison)
- **Wapens dragen** (carrying weapons) is also forbidden

### Scenario Rules
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| You or someone you know is victim of violence | Report to **Slachtofferhulp Nederland** or **politie** |
| You witness someone carrying a weapon | Report to **politie** |

## 4. De Scheiding van de Machten (Separation of Powers)

### The Three Powers
| Power | Dutch | Who | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive | **Uitvoerende macht** | **Regering**: Koning + Minister-president + Ministers | Executes laws and governs the country |
| Legislative | **Wetgevende macht** | **Parlement**: Tweede Kamer + Eerste Kamer | Makes new laws; controls the regering |
| Judicial | **Rechterlijke macht** | **Rechters** (judges) | Decides disputes; interprets the law |

### Key Facts
- The three powers work **los van elkaar** (independently of each other)
- Parliament may **not** tell a rechter what to decide
- A rechter may **not** vote on a new wetsvoorstel (legislative proposal)
- This system prevents any one power from gaining all control
- Together, the regering and parlement make new laws

### Kabinet
- **Kabinet** = all ministers + all **staatssecretarissen** (secretaries of state)
- Each minister has his/her own staatssecretaris who assists them

### Parlement in Detail
| Chamber | Dutch | Members | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower house | **Tweede Kamer** | **150** | Discusses government plans, can advise and propose new laws |
| Upper house | **Eerste Kamer** | **75** | Reviews work of the regering and Tweede Kamer |

## 5. Bestuur van Nederland (Governance Layers)

### Key Facts
- Nederland is a **koninkrijk** (kingdom) and a **democratie**
- There are **four levels** of government: nationaal → provinciaal → gemeentelijk → waterschappen
- The Netherlands is also part of the **Europese Unie**

### Levels of Government
| Level | Body | Leader |
|---|---|---|
| **National** (landelijk) | Parlement + Regering | Minister-president; Koning |
| **Provincial** | Provinciebestuur | **Commissaris van de koningin** |
| **Municipal** | **Gemeenteraad** | **Burgemeester** |
| **Water** | **Waterschappen** | — |

- Large municipalities (e.g. Amsterdam, Rotterdam) have **deelgemeenten** (partial municipalities), each with a **deelgemeenteraad**
- **Provincies** are responsible for **planologie** (spatial planning)

### Elections
| Concept | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Verkiezingen** | Elections |
| **Kiesrecht** | Right to vote |
| **Actief kiesrecht** | Right to vote in elections |
| **Passief kiesrecht** | Right to stand as a candidate and be voted for |
| **Tweede Kamer verkiezingen** | Most important national elections |
| **Stembureau** | Polling station (where you go to vote) |
| **Stembiljet** | Ballot paper |
| **Referendum** | Special election: only "yes" or "no" vote possible |

### After Elections: Forming a Government
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Meerderheid** | Majority |
| **Meerderheidspartijen** | Parties that together have a majority and form the government |
| **Coalitie** | The group of majority parties who form the government |
| **Oppositie** | Parties that did not form the government |
| **Lijsttrekker** | Leader of a political party during elections |

### Political Spectrum
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Rechts** | Right wing |
| **Links** | Left wing |
| **Progressief** | Progressive |
| **Conservatief** | Conservative |

## 6. Rechten en Plichten van Burgers

### Rights (Rechten)
| Right | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Recht op privacy** | Right to privacy |
| **Recht op uitkering** | Right to a benefit when unemployed or ill |
| **Recht op rechtsbijstand** | Right to legal assistance — you can get a lawyer |
| **Recht op onderwijs en zorg** | Right to education and healthcare |

### Duties (Plichten)
| Duty | Detail |
|---|---|
| Houden aan de wet | Must follow the law |
| Belasting betalen | Must pay taxes |
| Veranderingen doorgeven | Must report important changes: verhuizing, change in inkomen, change in gezinssituatie |
