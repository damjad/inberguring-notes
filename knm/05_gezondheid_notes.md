## 1. De Huisarts (General Practitioner)

### Gezonde Levensstijl (Healthy Lifestyle)
With a healthy lifestyle you need the GP less often. Tips from the SRT:
- Gezond eten en drinken (eat and drink healthily)
- Regelmatig bewegen en sporten (exercise regularly)
- Niet roken (don't smoke)
- Niet te veel alcohol drinken (don't drink too much alcohol)

### Key Facts
- Everyone in the Netherlands may choose their own GP (**huisarts**)
- Pick one close to your home; there is usually a **kennismakingsgesprek** (intake) first
- Dutch GPs are conservative with prescriptions — they prefer the body heals itself
- GPs have **beroepsgeheim** (professional secrecy) — cannot discuss your health with outsiders
- **Tandarts** (dentist): visit 2× per year for routine **controle** (check-up), even with no complaints
- Children under 18: dental care is covered free of charge
- Adults: dental care is **NOT** in the basic insurance — you need a separate **tandartsverzekering**

### Scenario Rules
| Situation                                     | Action                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Sick / feeling unwell, *not improving*        | Call huisarts, make appointment (**afspraak maken**)                                                               |
| Quick question, no appointment needed         | Call during **telefonisch spreekuur**                                                                              |
| Too old/sick to travel                        | GP does a **huisbezoek** (home visit)                                                                              |
| Chronic stable condition, mental health issue | **Praktijkondersteuner or POH** (practice nurse). They work under GP. But for regular check ups, you contact them. |
| Can't speak Dutch at appointment              | Contact a bilingual friend or family. If no one is available,  **Tolkencentrum** for an interpreter                |
| Unhappy with your GP                          | First talk to them; if not resolved → find a different huisarts                                                    |
| Can't go to your appointment                  | Cancel at least **24 hours** before, or you must still pay                                                         |
| Prescription received                         | Take **recept** to the **apotheek** (pharmacy)                                                                     |
| Simple meds (paracetamol, hoestdrank)         | Buy without prescription at apotheek, **drogist** (Etos, Kruidvat), or supermarkt                                  |
| Prescription meds                             | Only available at **apotheek** (not at drogist)                                                                    |

### Key Numbers
- Cancel appointment: ≥ 24 hours in advance
- Dentist check-ups: 2× per year

## 2. Ziekenhuis en Specialist (Hospital & Specialist)

### Key Facts
- Always go to GP first → GP writes a **verwijsbrief** (referral letter) to specialist
- In hospital: go to the **polikliniek** (specialized department, e.g. cardiologie, urologie)
- At first hospital visit: get a **patiëntenpas** made at the **balie** (reception)
- Verwijsbrief contains: your **BSN**, birthdate, reason for referral, specialist/institution name
- Verwijsbrief can be sent digitally (via Zorgdomein) or given to you on paper
- After treatment, the specialist informs your GP
- **huisartsenpost** is a GP in the hospital. You call them when your GP is unavailable (after hours, public holiday)

### What to Bring to the Polikliniek
1. **Verwijsbrief** from GP
2. **Verzekeringsbewijs** (insurance certificate)
3. **Identiteitsbewijs** (ID card / passport / driving licence)

### Scenario Rules
| Situation                                                                       | Action                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Referred by GP                                                                  | Make your own appointment with the specialist (call polykliniek or check website)                                                                        |
| Need to stay in hospital ≥1 day                                                 | That is called an **opname** (hospitalization)                                                                                                           |
| Mental/psychological complaints                                                 | GP → **GGZ** (Geestelijke Gezondheidszorg) or **maatschappelijk werk**                                                                                   |
| Sexual health issues such as Seksueel Overdraagbare Aandoening (SOA (eng: STD)) | Go directly to **GGD** (Gemeentelijke Gezondheidsdienst) — no GP referral needed                                                                         |
| After 17:00 / weekend, need a doctor                                            | Call your normal GP number → voicemail gives you **huisartsenpost** number → have **verzekeringsbewijs** ready (doctor will ask your verzekeringsnummer) |
| Life-threatening emergency                                                      | Call **112**                                                                                                                                             |
| Broken arm / bleeding wound                                                     | Go to **spoedeisende hulp** (ER or First Aid) — no appointment needed, most urgent treated first                                                         |

### Key Numbers / Contacts
| | |
|---|---|
| Emergency | **112** (also for police & fire brigade) |
| After-hours GP | **Huisartsenpost** (number on GP voicemail) |
| ER walk-in | **Spoedeisende hulp** = **Eerste hulp** |

## 3. Ouderen, Chronisch Zieken en Gehandicapten

### Key Facts
- Dutch policy: elderly and disabled live **independently as long as possible**
- If independent living becomes difficult → first contact GP or **WMO-loket** of gemeente

### Types of Care
| Type                               | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Thuiszorg**                      | Home care (help with shopping, household tasks like cleaning, transportation) provided by gemeente under WMO or wijkteam                                                                                                                         |
| **Mantelzorg**                     | Informal care by family/friends. Unpaid.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Dagbesteding**                   | Day activities for elderly/disabled (social activities, hobbies) like crafts, workshops, etc. <br>Arranged by WMO or wijkteam (neighbourhood team) for elderly who stay at home or lonely. Also, to give break to family members during the day. |
| **Particuliere thuiszorg**         | Hired private home care (paid professional, not family)                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Verzorgingshuis / Verpleeghuis** | Nursing home — when person can no longer care for themselves                                                                                                                                                                                     |

### How Care is Paid
| Law/System                                   | Covers                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **WLZ** (Wet Langdurige Zorg)                | 24-hour care needs for the people who need checking in every couple of hours such as dementia patients. They are normally put in Verpleeghuis. |
| **WMO** (Wet Maatschappelijke Ondersteuning) | Home help: cleaning, transport — via gemeente                                                                                                  |
| **Zorgverzekering**                          | Health Insurance: GP, wijkverpleging, hospital                                                                                                 |

### Key Organisations
| Organisation                             | Role                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **CIZ** (Centrum Indicatiestelling Zorg) | Assesses whether you qualify for WLZ care             |
| **CAK** (Centraal Administratiekantoor)  | Calculates your **eigen bijdrage** for long-term care |
| **Gemeente / Wijkteam**                  | Responsible for WMO help; apply via WMO-loket         |

### Scenario Rules
| Situation                                                         | Action                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Elderly struggling to live alone                                  | Contact huisarts or WMO-loket van gemeente                                              |
| Need 24-hour care                                                 | Apply for **WLZ indicatie** via **CIZ**. **CAK** will calculate your **eigen bijdrage** |
| Need home help (thuiszorg)(cleaning/transport)                    | Apply via gemeente / **sociaal wijkteam**                                               |
| Disabled, need adapted home<br>e.g. old knees -> automated stairs | Contact gemeente for **woonaanpassing** (e.g. traplift, douchestoel)                    |

## 4. Zorgverzekering (Health Insurance)

### Key Facts
- **Mandatory** for everyone living in the Netherlands: **basisverzekering**
- People 18+ pay a monthly **premie** (premium); children under 18 are free
- The government determines the **basispakket** each year (same for all insurers)
- Switch deadline: cancel by **31 December**, switch by **1 February**
- Compare insurers in November/December at: **independer.nl**, **zorgkiezer.nl**, **consumentenbond.nl**
- Most costs are paid **automatically** by insurer directly to huisarts/ziekenhuis/apotheek — you don't need to do anything

### What IS Covered (Basispakket) (full or partial (eigen risico or eigen bijdrage))
- Huisarts, ziekenhuis, most medicines
- GGZ (Geestelijke Gezondheidszorg) (mental health care), kraamzorg (maternity care), wijkverpleging (district nurse)
- Ambulancevervoer (ambulance)
- Ouderenzorg, revalidatie (rehabilitation)

### What is NOT Covered at all(for adults)
- **Tandarts** (dentist)
- **Fysiotherapie** (physiotherapy)
- Brillen (glasses), alternatieve zorg, orthodontie

> For children under 18: tandarts and fysiotherapie **ARE** covered.

### Extra Insurance Options
| Type | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Aanvullende verzekering** | Covers physio, glasses, etc. — not mandatory |
| **Tandartsverzekering** | Dental care — not mandatory, extra cost |

### Costs
| Term               | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Premie**         | Monthly insurance payment                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Eigen risico**   | First €385 of yearly costs you pay yourself; lower premie = higher eigen risico. When you pay this costs. The remaining costs are paid by the insurance.<br><br>Eigen risico examples: Hospital emergency visit, Blood tests, Standard prescription meds. etc. |
| **Eigen bijdrage** | Fixed contribution for specific items (e.g. certain medicines)<br><br>Eigen bijdrage examples: Brand name medication, Maternity Care (Kraamzorg - home visits), Hospital birth without reason, Medical devices (e.g. 75% gov, 25% you) etc. <br>               |
| **Zorgtoeslag**    | Government allowance for low-income earners (from Belastingdienst)                                                                                                                                                                                             |

### Scenario Rules
| Situation                        | Action                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Need to claim costs yourself     | Fill in **formulier teruggave** → **declareren** (to claim health costs) via insurer app/website or DigiD. |
| Low income → can't afford premie | Apply for **zorgtoeslag** at www.belastingdienst.nl                                                        |
| Want to switch insurer           | Cancel by **31 Dec** → switch by **1 Feb**                                                                 |
| Psychological care               | Need referral from GP or basis-GGZ to get reimbursement                                                    |

## 5. Digitale Vaardigheden en DigiD

### Key Facts
- **DigiD** = personal digital identity code from the Dutch government
- DigiD is **personal** — never share it with others

### What You Use DigiD For (Health-Related)
- Declare zorgkosten at your insurer
- View medical data (dossier, lab results)
- Make appointments with GP or specialist
- Request repeat prescriptions (**herhaal recepten**)
- Request kraamzorg or medical equipment
- Change/check zorgverzekeringspolis

### How to Get DigiD
1. Go to **www.digid.nl**
2. Fill in: BSN, geboortedatum, email, telefoonnummer
3. Confirm via SMS or DigiD app
4. Receive **activatiecode** by post within a few days
5. Activate DigiD

### Where to Get Help with DigiD
| Place | Help available |
|---|---|
| **Bibliotheek** | Free help with DigiD and digital healthcare |
| **Sociaal wijkteam** | Support for toeslagen and zorg |
| **Gemeente** | Help with DigiD, zorgtoeslag, voorzieningen |
| **Zorgverzekeraar** | Explains app and online declareren |

## 6. Welzijns- en Preventiefaciliteiten (Welfare & Prevention)

### What the Gemeente Helps With (via WMO)
- Geldproblemen (schulden, armoede) (debt, poverty)
- Eenzaamheid en sociaal isolement (Loneliness and social isolation)
- Deelname aan de samenleving (via vrijwilligerswerk) - Participation in society (through volunteer work)
- **Schuldhulpverlening** — help with debts/budgetbeheer
- **Huishoudelijke hulp** — cleaning, laundry, tidying
- **Woonaanpassingen** — traplift, douchestoel, scootmobiel
- **Mantelzorgondersteuning** — support for Informal carers (give the carer a break etc. by providing nurse or other carers for some duration)
- **Dagbesteding** — day activities for elderly/disabled
- Activiteiten in **buurthuizen** (community centres)

> **Hulp is vaak gratis of met een kleine bijdrage** — most municipal help is free or costs only a small contribution.

### How to Apply for Municipal Help
1. Contact **sociaal wijkteam** or **WMO-loket** van gemeente
2. Request a **keukentafelgesprek** (assessment conversation — literally "kitchen table talk")
3. Explain your problems and needs.
4. Receive a **beschikking** (official decision) from the gemeente
5. Help is arranged, usually via a zorgorganisatie (Care organisation)

> Anyone in the Netherlands can apply, **including non-Dutch nationals**.

### Who Can Help You Apply
- Sociaal wijkteam
- Gemeente (WMO-consulent)
- Bibliotheek (Informatiepunt Digitale Overheid)
- Welzijnsorganisaties: **Humanitas**, **Leger des Heils** (Welfare Organisations)
- Vrijwilligers- en mantelzorgsteunpunten (Volunteer and informal care centers)

## 7. Zwangerschap en Bevalling (Pregnancy & Birth)

### Key Facts
- Pregnant women go to the **verloskundige** (midwife) for regular check-ups, advice, and delivery (birth)
- Complications → referred to **gynaecoloog** → must give birth in **ziekenhuis**
- A **kraamhulp** comes to your home for one week after birth — you must arrange this yourself (via a **bureau voor kraamhulp**)
- Baby items: sometimes covered by insurance, or can be borrowed from thuiszorg

### Consultatiebureau (Child Health Clinic)
- For children aged **0–4 years**
- What is checked: height, lungs, reflexes, eyes, hearing, development (**ontwikkeling**) -> (senses, physical attributes, reflexes)
- Development is recorded in the **groeiboekje**.
- Advice given on: slapen (sleep), spelen (play), voeding (nutrition)
- Children also receive **vaccinaties** (vaccinations) here.

### Scenario Rules
| Situation                      | Action                                                |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Pregnant                       | Find a **verloskundige** near you and contact them.   |
| Complications during pregnancy | Referred to **gynaecoloog**, give birth in ziekenhuis |
| After birth                    | Arrange **kraamhulp** (1 week at home)                |
| Child aged 0–4                 | Attend **consultatiebureau** for regular check-ups    |
