## 1. Zorgen voor Jonge Kinderen

### Zwangerschap (Pregnancy)
| Situation | Who to contact |
|---|---|
| Normal pregnancy | **Verloskundige** directly (no GP referral needed) |
| Complications during pregnancy | **Gynaecoloog** (at the ziekenhuis) |

- Verloskundige does: **controles**, geeft **adviezen**, helps met de **bevalling**
- Baby born at home: you need certain items for the bevalling — can borrow from **thuiszorg** or get reimbursed by your insurance

### Consultatiebureau (0–4 jaar)
- Go here **after the birth** of your child
- What they do: controleert gezondheid, **meet en weegt** het kind, volgt **ontwikkeling** (talking, growing)
- Gives: **adviezen** over slapen en eten; **vaccinaties**
- Gemeente decides eligibility for VVE often based on advice of the consultatiebureau

### Kinderopvang Types
| Type | Age | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Kinderdagverblijf** | 0–4 jaar | Open ochtend tot avond; kinderen eten, spelen en slapen er |
| **Oppas** | Any | Family/neighbour/paid babysitter; comes to your home |
| **Gastouderopvang** | 0–12 jaar | Very **flexible** hours; child goes to gastouder's home OR gastouder comes to child's home |
| **BSO** (buitenschoolse opvang) | 4–12 jaar | Before/after school and during vakanties; only for basisschoolleerlingen; children picked up from school |
| **VVE** (Voorschoolse Educatie) | 2,5–4 jaar | Extra support for children behind in language/development; at peuterspeelzaal or kinderdagverblijf; gemeente decides eligibility |

### Kinderopvangtoeslag
- Childcare is expensive → you can get money back from the **belastingdienst**
- Apply at: **www.toeslagen.nl** → Mijn Toeslagen → log in with **DigiD**
- Fill in: type of opvang, aantal uren per maand, inkomensgegevens
- Amount depends on: **inkomen**, **aantal kinderen**, **aantal opvanguren**
- **Requirements**: parents must **work or study** AND opvang must be registered in the **landelijk register kinderopvang**
- Response within a few weeks from belastingdienst
- **Important**: if inkomen or uren change → report immediately, otherwise you must **terugbetalen**

## 2. Het Nederlandse Schoolsysteem

### Early Education (2–4 jaar)
| Type | For whom | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Peuterspeelzaal** | 2–4 jaar | 2–3 dagdelen per week; learn to play and speak Dutch |
| **Voorschool** | 2–4 jaar (not verplicht) | For children behind in development or who don't speak Dutch at home; extra language support |

### Basisschool (4–12 jaar)
| Groep | Age | What children learn |
|---|---|---|
| Groep 1–2 | 4–6 | Spelen, beter Nederlands, zingen, tekenen |
| Groep 3 | 6–7 | Lezen, schrijven, rekenen |
| Groep 4–8 | 7–12 | Aardrijkskunde, geschiedenis, muziek + more |

- At the end of **groep 8**: **citotoets** → gives an **advies** for which middelbare school level suits the child

### Middelbare School (12–18 jaar) — 3 Levels
| Level | Dutch | Duration | What follows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Highest | **VWO** | **6 jaar** | → **Universiteit** (dokter, advocaat) |
| Middle | **HAVO** | **5 jaar** | → **HBO** (e.g. leraar) |
| Practical | **VMBO** | **4 jaar** | → **MBO** (e.g. winkelier, kinderdagverblijf) |

### After Middelbare School
| Institution | Dutch | Entry level |
|---|---|---|
| **Universiteit** | Highest; doctor, lawyer | After **VWO** |
| **HBO** | Second highest; teacher etc. | After **HAVO** |
| **MBO** | Practical profession | After **VMBO** |

### Key Characteristics of Dutch Education
| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Vrijheid van onderwijs** | Parents choose school; anyone may start their own school (christelijk, islamitisch, openbaar, Dalton, Jena Plan) |
| **Leerplicht** | From **age 5**; mandatory until **age 16**; if no diploma at 16/17 → until **age 18** |
| **School absences** | Only allowed during official **schoolvakanties**; otherwise parents get a **boete**; special cases (begrafenis) → ask school for permission |
| **Speciaal onderwijs** | Schools specifically for children with a physical/mental handicap or learning difficulties |
| **Seksuele voorlichting** | **Verplicht** onderdeel in basis en voortgezet onderwijs |
| **Handicap facilities** | Most regular schools have facilities for pupils with a handicap |

## 3. Schoolkosten en Studiefinanciering

### Costs Overview
| Cost | Mandatory? | Who pays / Where to get |
|---|---|---|
| Basic schooling (tot 18 jaar) | — | **Free** |
| Schoolboeken (voortgezet onderwijs) | — | **Free** (VWO, HAVO, VMBO) |
| Schriften, rekenmachine | Verplicht | Parents |
| Excursies, schoolfeesten | Not mandatory | **Vrijwillige ouderbijdrage** (expected but not required) |

### Kinderbijslag
- Money from the **overheid** until child is **18 jaar** (for clothing, food, school costs)
- Paid by: **SVB** (Sociale Verzekeringsbank) — **NOT** the belastingdienst
- Amount depends on the **leeftijd** of the child

### Studiefinanciering (for higher education)
- For: **MBO level 3 and 4**, **HBO**, **Universiteit**
- Apply at: **DUO** via **www.duo.nl** — log in with DigiD
- Fill in: naam opleiding, startdatum, inkomensgegevens
- Types available:
  | Type | Details |
  |---|---|
  | **Lening** | Must be paid back later |
  | **Aanvullende beurs** | Grant if parents earn little; does not need to be repaid |
  | **OV-kaart** | Free public transport — either weekdays or weekends |
- Response within a few weeks from DUO
- **Important**: if something changes (e.g. stop studying) → report immediately → otherwise you must terugbetalen

### Help for Low-Income Families
| Organisation | What they help with |
|---|---|
| **Stichting Leergeld** | Children from families with little money: schoolspullen, sportclubs, muziekles, excursies |
| **Gemeentelijke regelingen** | Laptops, fietsen, kindkleding; check gemeente website or wijkteam |

## 4. Verantwoordelijkheid voor Kinderen

### Juridische Verantwoordelijkheid
| Age | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| 0–16 jaar | Parents are **juridisch verantwoordelijk** for all damage/behaviour |
| 18 jaar | **Volwassen** — can sign contracts independently |

### When Parents Need Help with Upbringing
| Situation | Where to go |
|---|---|
| General parenting problems | **Huisarts** or **Bureau Jeugdzorg** |
| Serious mental/psychological issues | **GGZ** (Geestelijke Gezondheidszorg) |

### School Registration — How to Register a Child
1. Choose a school nearby
2. Request an **inschrijfformulier** from the school
3. Fill in: naam, adres, BSN-nummer, ID, vaccinaties
4. Sometimes: intake gesprek with school directeur
5. Receive a **bevestiging** of your registration
- Deadline: in most gemeenten **before the child is 3 jaar and 10 maanden** (before the 4th birthday)
- In large cities: **voorrangsysteem** — register on time to have a chance

### Leerplicht — Parental Obligations
- Leerplicht starts at **age 5**; child must be present every **schooldag**
- Parents must ensure child arrives **on time**
- Report absence to school when child is sick
- **Only valid reasons** for absence: e.g. begrafenis familielid
- Too often late or absent → school reports to **leerplichtambtenaar**

### Parental Responsibilities Overview
| Domain | What parents are responsible for |
|---|---|
| **Behaviour** | At school, in the neighbourhood (op straat), on social media |
| **Health & Safety** | Veiligheid, gezondheid, opvoeding, good habits |
| **School support** | Help and guidance with schoolwork |
| **School involvement** | Attend ouderavonden, read rapporten, attend informatieavonden |

### Parent-School Contact Types
| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Ouderavond** | Evening where parents receive information from school |
| **10-minutengesprekken** | Brief one-on-one with the leraar to discuss your child |
| **Rapportavond** | Evening to discuss child's rapportcijfers with the docent |

- School may also ask parents to **volunteer** (vrijwilliger) for: schoolreisjes, sportdagen, in the classroom, ouderraad, medezeggenschapsraad
- Child does better at school when parents **show interest**, talk with the child about school, and work with the leraar

### Scenario Rules
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Child causes damage (before age 16) | **Parents** must pay |
| Child has developmental problems aged 2,5–4 | Contact **consultatiebureau** → apply for **VVE** via gemeente |
| Parenting problems at home | Contact **huisarts** or **bureau jeugdzorg** |
| Child absent from school too often | School reports to **leerplichtambtenaar** |
| Need to register child for school | Do it **before 3 jaar and 10 maanden** |
| Need financial help for school costs | **Stichting Leergeld** or **gemeentelijke regelingen** |
| Need studiefinanciering | Apply at **DUO** via www.duo.nl |
| Need kinderopvangtoeslag | Apply at **www.toeslagen.nl** with DigiD |
| Need kinderbijslag | Apply at **SVB** (not belastingdienst) |
