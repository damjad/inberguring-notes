## 1. De Gemeente (Municipality)

### Key Facts
- **Everyone** living in the Netherlands must register with their gemeente via the **GBA** (Gemeentelijke Basisadministratie) — also called **BRP** (Basisregistratie Personen), the modern official name for the same register. they give you **BSN** (Burgerservicenummer).
- You must notify your gemeente of life changes (see table below)
- Certain documents can **only** be obtained at the gemeente
- **Verblijfsvergunning** needed to live in the Netherlands; both tijdelijk and permanent are issued by the **IND** (Immigratie en Naturalisatiedienst) — the course material states you apply via the gemeente
- Permanent permit requires **≥ 5 years** of living in NL
- **Naturalisatie** (Dutch citizenship) can only be applied for once you have a permanent permit
- Low-income residents can apply for **ontheffing (exemption)** from municipal taxes

### Life Changes to Report to Gemeente
- start living in a house 
- changing house
- people living in the house
	- married / divorced
	- birth / death
	- moving in together etc.

| Change                               | Report to                |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Getting married / moving in together | Gemeente                 |
| Divorce                              | Gemeente                 |
| Having a child                       | Gemeente                 |
| Moving house                         | New gemeente you move to |
| Death of a family member             | Gemeente                 |

### Documents You Can Only Get at the Gemeente
- **Dutch Paspoort** (passport) — new or renewal
- **Dutch Identiteitskaart** (ID card) — new or renewal
- **Dutch Rijbewijs** (driver's licence)
- **Uittreksel GBA / BRP** — official document with your name, address, etc.
- **Vergunningen** (permits) — e.g. to renovate your home, cut a tree, add parking

### What to Bring When Applying
- Valid **paspoort** or **identiteitskaart**
- Old document if renewing
- Payment (most documents cost money)

### Verblijfsvergunning Types
| Type      | Dutch term               | Duration                             |
| --------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Temporary | Voor **bepaalde tijd**   | 1, 3, or 5 years                     |
| Permanent | Voor **onbepaalde tijd** | Indefinite (requires ≥5 years in NL) |

### Benefits of Naturalisatie
- Dutch passport
- No more verblijfsvergunning needed
- All rights and duties of a Dutch citizen

### Municipal Taxes (Gemeentebelasting)
- **Milieubelasting** (environmental tax)
- **Onroerend goedbelasting** (property tax)
- **Hondenbelasting** (dog tax)
- Low income → can apply for **ontheffing** (exemption)

### Scenario Rules
| Situation                            | Action                                                       |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Just arrived / moved to NL           | Register at gemeente (**BRP**) → get **BSN**                 |
| Moving to a new city                 | Notify **new** gemeente                                      |
| Want to renovate your home           | Apply for **vergunning** at gemeente                         |
| Want to cut a tree in your garden    | Apply for **vergunning** at gemeente                         |
| Want a parking space near your house | Apply for **vergunning** at gemeente                         |
| Have lived in NL ≥5 years            | Can apply for **permanente verblijfsvergunning** via **IND** |
| Have permanent permit                | Can apply for **naturalisatie** at **gemeente**              |

## 2. De Politie (Police)

### Key Facts
- **Identificatieplicht**: everyone over **14** must show ID when police request it
- In **public transport**: must show ID from age **12**
- Valid IDs: paspoort, ID-kaart, rijbewijs
- Paspoort, ID-kaart, rijbewijs are all valid for **10 years**
- **Always carry your rijbewijs** when driving — police can ask to see it
- You must **always cooperate** with the police — refusing can result in a **boete** or being taken to the bureau

### Travel with ID Documents
| Document  | Where can you travel?                            |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Paspoort  | Worldwide                                        |
| ID-kaart  | Europe only                                      |
| Rijbewijs | Cannot be used for travel. Used just for driving |

### Police Tasks
- **Hulpverlening** — providing help in emergencies (accidents, assault, missing persons)
- **Handhaving openbare orde** — maintaining public order
- Preventing and stopping **strafbare feiten** (criminal offences)
- **Verkeerscontrole** — traffic safety
- Neighbourhood supervision (**wijkagent**)
- Education at schools and events

### Wijkagent (Neighbourhood Officer)
- Every neighbourhood has this experienced police officer.
- Knows the area and local problems; prevents crime by being present
- Can be found at: **inloopspreekuur** (walk-in consultation hour), the police bureau, neighbourhood meetings, or www.politie.nl

### Phone Numbers
| Situation                          | Number        |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Emergency (urgent)                 | **112**       |
| Non-urgent (e.g. noisy neighbours) | **0900-8844** |
| Anonymous tip / crime report       | **0800-7000** |

### Scenario Rules
| Situation                        | Action                                                  |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Police ask for your ID (age ≥14) | Show ID — refusing = **boete** or taken to bureau       |
| Driving a car                    | Always carry **rijbewijs**                              |
| Wallet stolen / crime witnessed  | **Aangifte doen** at politiebureau or by phone          |
| Urgent police needed             | Call **112**                                            |
| Non-urgent issue (e.g. noise)    | Call **0900-8844**                                      |
| Want to report anonymously       | Call **0800-7000**                                      |
| Discrimination experienced       | → **Anti-discriminatiebureau**, or aangifte bij politie |

## 3. Belastingaangifte en Toeslagen (Tax Return & Allowances)

### Key Facts
- **Everyone** living in NL must pay belasting (tax)
- Tax funds: schools, police, roads, hospitals, healthcare
- Each person has a unique **BSN** (Burgerservicenummer) — on your passport and tax forms
- A **DigiD** code is needed to arrange tax matters online
- **Jaaropgave** (annual income statement from employer) — keep for **5 years** because Belastingdienst can retroactively reaccess or audit you.
- Aangifte period: **1 March – 1 May** (Maart and April) each year (via www.belastingdienst.nl)

### Types of Tax You Pay
| Tax           | Dutch                                                                                                                                                                                                | How paid                                        |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Income tax    | **Inkomstenbelasting**                                                                                                                                                                               | Deducted automatically from salary each month   |
| VAT           | **BTW**<br>21% for most goods and services (furniture, electronics, clothes, cars)<br>9% for groceries, food, books, medicines, museum tickets etc.<br>0% for healthcare, childcare, insurances etc. | Included in purchase prices                     |
| Road tax      | **Wegenbelasting**                                                                                                                                                                                   | Separate bill if you own a motor vehicle.       |
| Municipal tax | **Gemeentelijke belasting**                                                                                                                                                                          | Separate bill from gemeente — must pay yourself |

### Belastingaangifte Process
1. Collect your **jaaropgave** (annual statement from employer)
2. Go to **www.belastingdienst.nl** and log in with DigiD
3. Submit income + expenses between **1 March and 1 May**
4. Belastingdienst calculates if you get money back or owe more

### If You Can't Pay / Disagree
| Problem                    | Solution                                                |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Disagree with tax decision | **Bezwaar maken** (file objection) via online formulier |
| Can't pay all at once      | Request **in termijnen betalen** (installments)         |
| Very low income            | Request **kwijtschelding** (full exemption)             |
| Need help with forms       | Go to **belastingkantoor** in your area                 |

### Toeslagen (Allowances for Low Income)
You must apply for toeslagen yourself at belastingdienst.nl.

| Toeslag                 | For                             |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Huurtoeslag**         | Rent costs                      |
| **Zorgtoeslag**         | Healthcare insurance costs      |
| **Kinderopvangtoeslag** | Childcare costs (Daycare costs) |

Basically all allowances (toeslag) are applied via (toeslag.nl at belastingdienst) except ***Kinderbijslag***. It's applied via **SVB** (Sociale Verzekeringsbank).

## 4. Juridische en Sociale Hulp (Legal & Social Help)

### Key Organisations
| Organisation                 | What they do                                        | Cost                          |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Sociale raadslieden**      | Help writing letters, filling forms, giving advice  | **Free**                      |
| **Juridisch Loket**          | Free legal advice; can refer you to an advocaat     | **Free**                      |
| **Advocatenkantoor**         | Full legal representation for court procedures      | Paid (reduced for low income) |
| **Anti-discriminatiebureau** | Help in discrimination cases                        | Free                          |
| **Voedselbank**              | Free food packages                                  | Free (low income only)        |
| **Maatschappelijk werk**     | Help with relationship problems, loneliness, stress | Free/low cost                 |
| **Jeugdzorg**                | Support for parents and children                    | —                             |
| **Nibud**                    | Financial advice and budget tips                    | —                             |
| **Schuldhulpverlening**      | Help with debts and payment problems. (Gemeente)    | —                             |
| **GGD**                      | Health, vaccinations, sexual health                 | —                             |

### Legally Forbidden in the Netherlands
- Seks tussen volwassenen (adult) en iemand onder **16 jaar** (minderjaarige, minor)
- Verkrachting (rape), incest
- **Eerwraak** (honour violence)
- **Besnijdenis van meisjes** (female genital mutilation)
- Geweld en mishandeling (violence and abuse)
- **Discriminatie** is wettelijk verboden

### Scenario Rules
| Situation                          | Action                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Disagree with government decision  | **Bezwaar maken** → sociale raadslieden can help                                        |
| Need free legal advice             | → **Juridisch Loket**                                                                   |
| Need lawyer for court              | → **Advocatenkantoor** (via Juridisch Loket referral)                                   |
| Experienced discrimination         | → **Anti-discriminatiebureau** or politie aangifte (www.discriminatie.nl / www.art1.nl) |
| Have debts / can't pay bills       | → **Schuldhulpverlening** van gemeente                                                  |
| Problems with landlord or employer | → **Juridisch Loket**                                                                   |
| Don't have enough food             | → **Voedselbank**                                                                       |
| Problems with parenting / family   | → **Maatschappelijk werk** or **Jeugdzorg**                                             |

### How to Contact a Help Organisation
1. Find phone number or website
2. Call or fill in contact form
3. Make an appointment
4. Bring your **documenten** and **BSN-nummer**

## 5. Banken (Banks)

### Key Facts
- Almost everyone in NL has a **bankrekening** (bank account)
- Main banks: **ABN AMRO**, **ING**, **Rabobank** — you choose freely
- Opening an account: go to bank website → receive rekeningnummer + **bankpas** + **pincode**
- You can withdraw cash at ATMs (**geldautomaten**) from **any** bank, regardless of your own bank
- **Never share** your inloggegevens (login details) with others

### Payment Methods
| Method | Details |
|---|---|
| **Pincode** | Use with bankpas at shops or ATM |
| **Contactloos betalen** | Small amounts only; tap bankpas or phone/watch |
| **Machtiging** | Authorise a company to debit your account automatically (e.g. energy, rent, phone) |
| **Internetbankieren** | Transfer money online via app or website |
| **Betaalverzoek / Tikkie** | Send/receive payment requests via app or QR code |
| **Apple Pay / Google Pay** | Mobile payment |
| **Acceptgiro** | Paper payment card sent with bill — old-fashioned, rarely used now |

### If Your Bankpas is Lost or Stolen
1. **Block** your bankrekening immediately (call bank or go in person)
2. **Aangifte doen** bij de politie
3. Request a **nieuwe pas** at your bank

### Scenario Rules
| Situation                               | Action                                             |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Want to pay monthly bills automatically | Set up automatic payments via **machtiging**       |
| Bankpas lost or stolen                  | Block account → aangifte politie → request new pas |
| Want to send money to someone           | Use **internetbankieren** or **Tikkie**            |
| Want to pay in a shop without PIN       | Use **contactloos betalen** (small amounts only)   |

## 6. Verzekeringen (Insurances)

### Mandatory Insurances
| Insurance | Dutch | Mandatory? |
|---|---|---|
| Health insurance | **Zorgverzekering** (basisverzekering) | **Yes** |
| Car insurance | **Autoverzekering** | **Yes** (if you own a car) |

### Common Optional Insurances
| Insurance | Dutch | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Liability insurance | **Aansprakelijkheidsverzekering** | Damage you/your family cause to others |
| Fire insurance | **Brandverzekering** | Fire damage to your home |
| Travel insurance | **Reisverzekering** | Damage/loss while travelling |

### How to Claim (Schade Melden)
1. Go to the insurance company's website
2. Fill in a **schadeformulier** (damage form)
3. Provide an accurate description of the damage
- **Faking damage is strafbaar (illegal)**

## 7. Communicatie met de Overheid (Digital Government)

### Key Facts
- The Dutch government communicates increasingly online
- Use **DigiD** to log in to all government portals
- **BSN** is your personal government number — keep it **secret** (sensitive information)
- You get your BSN when registering at the gemeente
- **BRP** (Basisregistratie Personen) is the modern official name for the GBA — you can change your address registration and request official extracts via the BRP online

### Where You Use DigiD
| Institution         | For what                                                                |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gemeente**        | Paspoort/rijbewijs aanvragen, verhuizing doorgeven, uittreksel BRP      |
| **Belastingdienst** | Aangifte doen, toeslagen aanvragen                                      |
| **UWV**             | Work and uitkering (benefits)                                           |
| **DUO**             | Studiefinanciering (student finance)                                    |
| **Zorgverzekeraar** | Polis beheren (manage insurance policy), declareren (to declare claims) |
| **Huisartsportaal** | Appointments, repeat prescriptions                                      |

### Safety Rules for Digital Government
- Only log in via **official websites**: overheid.nl, toeslagen.nl, belastingdienst.nl
- **Never share** your wachtwoord (password) or SMS-code
- Lock your phone/computer with a code

### Where to Get Help with Digital Matters
| Place                         | Help available                                                       |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bibliotheek**               | Informatiepunt Digitale Overheid — help with DigiD, toeslagen, forms |
| **Sociaal wijkteam**          | Help applying for voorzieningen (provisions)                         |
| **Gemeente**                  | Sometimes has special spreekuur for digital help                     |
| **Vrijwilligersorganisaties** | Volunteer organisations e.g. Humanitas                               |
