### De Middeleeuwen (±500–1500)
- Area was called **de Nederlanden** — multiple small lands with their own leaders
- Most people were **boeren** (farmers) and were poor
- Roman cities still visible: **Utrecht** and **Maastricht** come from the Roman era (~year 0)

---

### De Tachtigjarige Oorlog (1568–1648)
- **Koning Filips II** of Spain was in charge of the Nederlanden
- Filips II was Catholic; wanted everyone to be Catholic; had much power; imposed heavy taxes
- Dutch people who **protested** against the Catholic church were called **protestanten**
- Filips II wanted all protestants to be killed
- 1568: protestants began fighting Filips II → start of the Tachtigjarige Oorlog
- Leader of the resistance: **Willem van Oranje**
  - Murdered in **1584**
  - Ancestor of King Willem Alexander
  - Named "Wilhelmus" in the national anthem **Het Wilhelmus**
- 1588: became the **Republiek der Zeven Verenigde Nederlanden** (wanted independence from Spain)
- 1648: end of war → republic became a **zelfstandige staat** (independent state)

---

### De Gouden Eeuw (1600–1700)
- Netherlands became very rich through **handel** (trade in goud and specerijen)
- 1602: state founded the **VOC** (Verenigde Oost-Indische Compagnie) — hugely successful trading company in Asia
- **Slavernij**: Dutch also transported and sold enslaved people from Africa; they worked on plantations in Suriname and the Antillen — a **donkere periode** in Dutch history
- Big gap between arm and rijk
- Important for kunst: **Rembrandt** — most famous schilderij: **De Nachtwacht**
- Rich people lived in **grachtenhuizen** (still visible in Amsterdam, Utrecht, Leiden)
- Kolonies acquired: **Indonesië, Suriname, Nederlandse Antillen, Zuid-Afrika, Sri Lanka**
- **Keti Koti**: herdenkt het einde van de slavernij; every year on **1 juli**
- Indonesië became independent: **1949**; Suriname: **1975**
- After decolonisation: many people from Suriname and Indonesië came to the Netherlands

---

### De Industriële Revolutie (1800–1900)
- Machines, fabrieken, nieuwe uitvindingen
- People moved to cities to work → called **arbeiders**
- Women and children also worked in factories; long working days; very little pay
- First political parties founded — mainly **socialisten**
  - Wanted better living conditions for arbeiders
  - Wanted equal rights for everyone (poor/rich, man/vrouw)
- First **grondwet** (constitution): **1814**

---

### De Tweede Wereldoorlog (1940–1945)
| Event | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1930s Germany | Many poor factory workers; Hitler becomes leader |
| Hitler's propaganda | Blamed Jews for Germany's problems |
| 1940 | Germany **occupied** the Netherlands |
| Koningin Wilhelmina | Fled to England with the royal family — too dangerous |
| From 1942 | Jews rounded up; sent to **concentratiekampen** |
| Dutch Jewish casualties | **More than 75%** of Dutch Jews died in the camps |
| **Het verzet** | Resistance: helped Jews hide (onderduiken), spread news about the war |
| Liberation | **1945** — Netherlands liberated by England, USA, Canada |

### Recente Geschiedenis (1945 – heden)
- **1945–1955**: Reconstruction — houses and factories rebuilt; economy recovered
- **1949**: NAVO founded — military cooperation between European countries, USA, and Canada
- **1953**: Grote Overstroming — Zeeland, Zuid-Holland, Noord-Brabant flooded; **1836 doden**
  - Led to: **Deltaplan** → government plan to protect against water
  - Executed as: **Deltawerken** — Netherlands' largest water defense system (Noordzee)
  - Also: **Afsluitdijk** (see Geography section)
- **1957**: Start of European economic cooperation → became the **EU**; euro introduced **2002**
- **1960s–1970s**: More personal freedom
  - Women working and studying more
  - **Ontkerkelijking**: church became less influential in Dutch society
  - ~1970: acceptance of **homosexualiteit**
  - Abortus permitted under certain conditions
  - **Homohuwelijk** legally recognised since **2001** — Netherlands was **first in the world**
  - **Euthanasie** permitted under strict conditions since **2002**
- **1960s–1970s**: **Gastarbeiders** arrived from **Turkije** and **Marokko** (economy was booming, lots of work); many stayed permanently
- Also immigrants from former colonies (Indonesië, Suriname, Antillen) and **vluchtelingen** (Iraq, Syria, Afghanistan, Eritrea)
- Netherlands has become a multicultural society

---

### UNESCO Wereld Erfgoed
- Cultural or natural heritage that is unique and irreplaceable; important for the whole world
- Goal: protect this heritage for future generations
- UNESCO Wereld Erfgoed verdrag: **1972**
- In 2021: **1154** items on the list
  - **897** cultureel erfgoed (buildings, cities)
  - **288** natuurlijk erfgoed (nature areas)
  - **39** gemengd erfgoed (combination)
- Each country is responsible for protecting its own erfgoed

## 2. Geografie van Nederland

### Location and Size
- **West-Europa**
- Neighbours: **België** (south), **Duitsland** (east); North Sea (west/north)
- Size: **300 km** long, **150 km** wide
- "Nederland" = **Laagland** (low land) — **27% lies below sea level**

### Provincies and Major Cities
- **12 provincies**, each with its own hoofdstad
- Key exam fact: know where the 12 provinces are on the map

| City | Rank | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Amsterdam** | Capital | Biggest airport: **Schiphol** |
| **Rotterdam** | 2nd | Biggest haven in Europe: **De Europoort** |
| **Den Haag** | 3rd | Seat of **regering en parlement** (political heart) |
| **Utrecht** | 4th | — |

- Noord-Holland hoofdstad: **Haarlem**; Zuid-Holland hoofdstad: **Den Haag**

### De Randstad
- **Amsterdam + Rotterdam + Den Haag + Utrecht**
- Located in the **west** of the Netherlands
- Many bedrijven; most work opportunities in the country

### Natural Areas
| Area | Location | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **De Veluwe** | Province Gelderland (east) | Large nature area; quiet |
| **De Waddeneilanden** | North | Chain of islands in the north |
| **Flevoland** | Centre | Province almost entirely made of **polders** |

### Water Management
- **27%** of Netherlands lies below sea level → Netherlands has centuries of water management history
- Protection methods: **duinen, dijken, molens, polders**

| Water feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Polder** | Land that used to be water; artificially drained and made into land |
| **IJsselmeer** | Biggest meer in NL; used to be a sea until the Afsluitdijk was built |
| **Afsluitdijk** | Dike from **Noord-Holland** to **Friesland**; also a road you can drive on |
| **Deltawerken** | Largest water defense system; mainly in Zeeland; against Noordzee |
| **Deltaplan** | Government plan after 1953 flood that led to the Deltawerken |

### Klimaat
| Season | Temperature | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| Zomer | ±**18°C** | Long days |
| Herfst | ±**10°C** | Much wind and rain |
| Winter | ±**5°C** (can drop below 0) | Can snow |
| Lente | ±**12°C** | Trees get new leaves |

- **Zeeklimaat**: winters not cold, summers not very hot

## 4. Openbaar Vervoer

### Key Facts
- Types: **bus, tram, metro, trein**
- OV is **toegankelijk voor iedereen**
- Usually **goedkoper en duurzamer** than travelling by auto

### OV-chipkaart
| Type | Details |
|---|---|
| **Persoonlijk** | Has your naam and foto |
| **Anoniem** | Without naam; no ID required |
- Always **inchecken** at the start and **uitchecken** at the end of every journey
- Ensure there is sufficient **saldo** or a valid abonnement on the card

### Abonnementen
| Abonnement | Discount |
|---|---|
| **Daluren korting** | **40% off** during off-peak hours (buiten de spits) |
| **Weekendvrij** | Free travel in the weekend |
| **Altijdvrij** | Free travel at all times |
| **Voordeelurenabonnement** | Discount on treinreizen |
| **Kinderen tot 12** | Up to **3 children** free per adult |
| **65-plus** | Discount at some regional buses/trams — not all OV |

### How to Buy a Ticket (No Abonnement)
| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Loskaartje** | Via automaat or servicebalie (not at all stations) |
| **9292 app** | All OV types (bus, tram, metro, trein); QR code to check in |
| **NS app** | Trein only; QR code to check in |
| **Contactloos PIN/creditcard** | Check in and out contactlessly via OVpay |

### Planning Apps
| App | For |
|---|---|
| **9292.nl** | All types of OV |
| **NS app** | Trein only |
- Both show: **vertrektijden, overstappen, kosten, vertragingen**
