## 1. Werk Zoeken (Finding Work)

### Key Facts
- A **vacature** (job posting) lists: functie (position), functie-eisen (requirements), werktijden, and sometimes salaris
- You must send a **CV** + **sollicitatiebrief** or fill in an online **sollicitatieformulier**
- The government expects you to **actively seek work** — work is your own responsibility

### Where to Find Vacatures
| Source              | Where                                                                |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **UWV Werkbedrijf** | www.werk.nl                                                          |
| **Job sites**       | www.nationalevacaturebank.nl, www.vakant.nl, indeed.nl, linkedin.com |
| **Uitzendbureau**   | Employment Agency. Register yourself or apply directly to a vacancy  |

### Uitzendbureau (Temp Agency)
- Usually offers **tijdelijk werk** (contract voor bepaalde tijd)
- Often sector-specific: **horeca** (Hotel, Restaurant, Café), administratie, gezondheidszorg
- You can register and wait for contact, or apply directly to a vacancy

### Key Qualities Employers Look For
- **Zelfstandig werken** — work independently
- **Samenwerken** — teamwork
- **Initiatieftonen** — show initiative
- **Betrouwbaarheid** — reliability (on time, keeps agreements)

### Language Requirements for Work
- Different jobs require different Dutch levels: **A2, B1, or B2**
- Employers expect you to read instructions, hold client conversations, and collaborate
- Without Dutch it is very hard to find or keep a job

### Scenario Rules
| Situation                                     | Action                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Looking for a job                             | Search daily on werk.nl / nationalevacaturebank.nl; set up a **vacature alert** |
| Applying                                      | Send **CV + sollicitatiebrief** or fill in online **sollicitatieformulier**     |
| Want temp/flexible work                       | Register at an **uitzendbureau**                                                |
| Struggling with Dutch                         | Follow **NT2** lessons at taalschool or ROC; find a **taalmaatje** via gemeente |
| Don't know what your foreign diploma is worth | Go to **IDW** for diploma recognition                                           |
| Have relevant work experience from abroad     | Go to **EVC** for an official certificate of competence                         |

## 2. Diploma's, Cursussen en Bijscholing

### Key Facts
- Most jobs in NL require a **diploma**
- Foreign diplomas can be assessed at **IDW** (Informatiedienst Werk)
- **DUO** can provide a **lening** (loan) to fund a study or course
- **ROC** (Regionaal Opleidingscentrum) offers **beroepsopleidingen** (vocational training, e.g. verpleegster(nurse))
- **Reintegratiebedrijven** help people who have been unemployed for a longer period find new work
- **MAP** (Module Arbeidsmarkt en Participatie) is a **mandatory** training for newcomers as part of inburgering

### Types of Extra Training
| Term                 | Meaning                                                    |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bijscholing**      | Learning new things to qualify for a specific job          |
| **Nascholing**       | Specialisation training to advance in a career             |
| **Beroepsopleiding** | Vocational training for a specific profession. Done at ROC |
| **NT2**              | Dutch language course for non-native speakers              |

### Popular Courses
- NT2 taallessen (Dutch language)
- Communicatie (samenwerken, klantgerichtheid)
- Vakspecifieke trainingen (logistiek, zorg)
- Techniek (lassen, installatietechniek)

### Where to Follow Courses
| Provider | What |
|---|---|
| **ROC** | Beroepsopleidingen, NT2 |
| **Gemeente / UWV** | Various courses and reintegration |
| **Taalschool / reintegratiebureau** | NT2, MAP training |
| **Online** | NTi, LOI, OpenOnderwijs, Duolingo, oefenen.nl |

### MAP — Module Arbeidsmarkt en Participatie
- Mandatory for all **nieuwkomers** (part of inburgering)
- Learn: how to find work in NL, what matters on the **werkvloer**, how to present yourself
- Followed at taalschool or reintegratiebureau
- You must actively participate and make appointments

## 3. Het Arbeidscontract (Employment Contract)

### What Is in Your Contract
- Salaris and vakantiegeld (Salary and holiday pay)
- Startdatum
- Uren per week (hours per week)
- Aantal vakantiedagen (number of vacation days)
- Proeftijd (if applicable) (probation period)
- What to do when sick
- Verlofregels (leave rules)

### Proeftijd (Probation Period)
- A trial period to test if the job and employee are a match
- **Both** sides can end the contract at any point during proeftijd without notice

### Contract Types
| Type                  | Details                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Vast / permanent**  | Onbepaalde tijd; employer cannot simply fire you; right to pensioen, vakantiegeld, doorbetaling bij ziekte; easier to get a mortgage/loan |
| **Tijdelijk**         | Bepaalde termijn (e.g. 6 months, 1 year); einddatum in contract; can be extended or become permanent                                      |
| **Via uitzendbureau** | Salary paid via uitzendbureau; flexible/tijdelijk; can be stopped per day or week; less pensioen build-up                                 |
| **Deeltijd**          | Fewer hours than voltijd; proportionally less pay and vakantiedagen; same arbeidsrechten as voltijd                                       |

### Voltijd vs Deeltijd
- **Voltijd** in NL = **36, 38, or 40 hours per week** (depending on sector)
- **Deeltijd** = fewer hours → less pay, fewer vakantiedagen, but **same labour rights**

### Sick Leave Rules
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Sick on a workday | Call in sick (ziek melden) — phone or email |
| Sick for a longer period | Report to **bedrijfsarts / arboarts** |
| Arboarts assessment | Investigates and creates a **reintegratieplan** |

### Arbowet
- Dutch law that sets safety and health rules at work
- Examples: good chairs in offices; helmets on construction sites
- Both employer and employee must follow the arbowet

## 4. Salaris, Belasting en CAO

### Bruto vs Netto
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bruto-salaris** | Your full salary before deductions (belasting + premies) |
| **Netto-salaris** | What you actually receive in your bank account |
| **Loonstrookje** | Monthly payslip showing deductions |
| **Jaaropgave** | Annual income statement (keep for 5 years) |

### Premies (Social Insurance Contributions)
- **AOW-verzekering** — pension when you reach retirement age (67)
- **WW-verzekering** — unemployment benefit if you lose your job
- **Pensioen** — additional workplace pension

### Lastenverdeling
- People with a **higher salary pay more belasting** than those with a lower salary
- This is called **lastenverdeling** (redistribution of burden)

### CAO (Collectieve Arbeidsovereenkomst) (Collective Employment Contract)
- Agreement between employers and employees in the **same sector**
- Differs per sector: bouw, schoonmaak, zorg, horeca
- If a CAO exists, it **overrides** your individual contract
- **Rights from CAO**: minimumloon, salaris bij ziekte, vakantie + vakantiegeld
- **Duties from CAO**: report illness, work contracted hours

### Vakbond (Trade Union)
- Every profession has its own vakbond
- Vakbonden negotiate with employers over: CAO, salarissen, werktijden, werkomstandigheden
- Can also help individual workers with problems at work

### Werknemersvertegenwoordiging (Employee Representation)

| Body                                                  | When required                    | Role                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ondernemingsraad (OR)** (Works Council)             | Companies with **>50** employees | Workers can vote on plans that affect them                                  |
| **Medezeggenschapsraad (MR)** (Participation council) | Companies with **<50** employees | Similar role to OR                                                          |
| **Personeelsvereniging** (Staff Association)          | Any size                         | Organises fun social events; colleagues can join and get to know each other |

## 5. Uitkeringen en Bijstand (Benefits)

### WW-Uitkering (Unemployment Benefit)
- For people who have **lost their job**
- Managed by **UWV**

### Requirements for WW
| Condition | Detail |
|---|---|
| Must be **werkloos** | You lost your job involuntarily |
| **Referte-eis** | Worked for more than **half a year** before becoming unemployed |
| Must be **insured for werkloosheid** | Covered via your employer |

### How to Apply for WW
1. Register at **UWV Werkbedrijf** online via **werk.nl** (log in with DigiD)
2. Do this **within 1 week** of your last working day
3. UWV also helps you search for a new job

### WW Obligations (Sollicitatieplicht)
- Actively search for work and **prove it to UWV**
- Accept offered work (even if not your dream job)
- Report changes in your situation (new job, income change, moving, samenwonen)
- Failing to report = **boete** or uitkering **verlaagd (reduced) / stopgezet (stopped)**

### Bijstand (Social Assistance — No Work History)
- For people who **never worked or worked too little** for WW
- Applied for at the **gemeente**
- Requirements: almost no income/savings; no right to WW or WIA; legal resident in NL
- Bijstand is **tijdelijk** — you must cooperate with pathways to werk or scholing

### Other Uitkeringen
| Uitkering    | For                                                        | Via      |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| **WW**       | Lost your job; worked ≥ half a year                        | UWV      |
| **WIA**      | Long-term unable to work (arbeidsongeschikt) -> Disability | UWV      |
| **AOW**      | Reached retirement age (**≥ 67**)                          | SVB      |
| **Bijstand** | No income / no work history                                | Gemeente |

### Toeslagen
You must apply for these yourself:

| Toeslag                 | For              | **Via**                        |
| ----------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Zorgtoeslag**         | Healthcare costs | toeslag.nl via belastingdienst |
| **Huurtoeslag**         | Rent costs       | toeslag.nl via belastingdienst |
| **Kindgebonden budget** | Children         | toeslag.nl via belastingdienst |

### When in Doubt
→ Ask **UWV** or **gemeente** for advice before applying

## 6. Discriminatie op het Werk

### Key Facts
- Discrimination = unfair treatment based on: naam, afkomst, huidskleur, leeftijd, geloof, kleding, geslacht, seksuele voorkeur, zwangerschap, handicap
- **Wettelijk verboden** (illegal) by Dutch law
- You have the right to: gelijke kansen bij sollicitatie, gelijke behandeling op de werkvloer, een veilige werkplek

### Steps to Take
| Step                        | Action                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Talk first               | Speak with the colleague, boss, or **HR** (personeelszaken)                                                          |
| 2. Vertrouwenspersoon       | Contact the workplace confidant                                                                                      |
| 3. Formal report            | → **Vakbond**, bedrijfsarts/arbodienst, meldpunt discriminatie bij gemeente                                          |
| 4. Independent body         | → **College voor de Rechten van de Mens** (free; online/phone; www.mensrechten.nl)                                   |
| 5. Anti-discriminatiebureau | → www.discriminatie.nl / www.art1.nl ADV is an independent organisation where one can report cases of descrimination |
| 6. Last resort              | **Aangifte doen bij de politie**                                                                                     |

### College voor de Rechten van de Mens
- Independent organisation that investigates discrimination complaints
- **Free** — online or by phone
- Issues an **oordeel** (judgement) — not legally binding but carries weight

## 7. Eigen Bedrijf / ZZP (Self-Employment)

### Key Facts
- All businesses must register with the **KvK** (Kamer van Koophandel) — you receive a **KVK-nummer**
- KvK also provides info on: belasting, ondernemingsplan, cursussen → www.kvk.nl
- As a ZZP you have **no employer** — you find your own clients, earn what you bring in, pay your own tax

### Common ZZP Professions
Kapper, klusjesman, loodgieter, vertaler, etc.

### What You Must Arrange Yourself as ZZP
| Responsibility                                                     | Detail                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pensioen**                                                       | Not built up automatically — must arrange yourself                          |
| **AOV** (Arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering) (Disability insurance) | If you become ill/disabled — no government coverage without your own AOV    |
| **Aansprakelijkheidsverzekering** (Liability Insurance)            | If you cause damage to a client                                             |
| **Boekhouding + belastingaangifte**                                | Keep records; file tax return (tip: hire a boekhouder or follow KvK course) |

### Starting a Business While on Uitkering
| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| On **WW** (via UWV) | Must ask **toestemming** (permission) first |
| On **bijstand** (via gemeente) | Must **melden** (notify) before starting |
| Proefperiode allowed | Up to ~6 months to try being an ondernemer |
| Start without notifying | **Uitkering stops + must repay money** |

### AOV Uitkering (ZZP Disability Benefit)
- If you become **arbeidsongeschikt** as a ZZP
- **NOT automatic** — you must have taken out an **AOV-verzekering** yourself
- Apply via your own insurer

### Financing Your Business
- Bank requires an **ondernemingsplan** (business plan) before granting a loan
- Ondernemingsplan describes: what you will do and how

### Pros and Cons of ZZP
| Voordelen               | Nadelen                                 |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Work independently      | No fixed income                         |
| Set your own hours      | Must bear risk of illness or no clients |
| Choose your own clients | Must handle everything yourself         |
| Can earn more when busy | No automatic pensioen or insurance      |

## 8. Het Sollicitatiegesprek (Job Interview)

### Preparation
1. Read the vacature carefully — understand what they're looking for
2. Prepare answers to key questions (see below)
3. Practise answers **out loud**
4. Dress representatively
5. Be on time and know where to go
6. Prepare **your own questions** for the interviewer

### Common Interview Questions
| Question                             | Tip                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Vertel ons iets over jezelf          | Prepare a short introduction            |
| Waarom wil je bij ons werken?        | Show you researched the company         |
| Wat zijn je sterke en zwakke punten? | Be honest, show self-awareness          |
| Heb je ervaring met dit werk?        | Link your past experience to this role  |
| Hoe werk je samen met collega's?     | Give an example of teamwork             |
| Wat verwacht je van deze baan?       | Be realistic and positive               |
| Heb je nog vragen?                   | Always prepare 1–2 questions in advance |
